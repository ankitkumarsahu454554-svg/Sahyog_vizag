Disaster Survival & First-Aid Manual

Essential field guides for cyclones (Hudhud-grade storm response), urban flash floods, and life-critical first aid.

Cyclone Survival Rules (విశాఖ తుపాను జాగ్రత్తలు)
  • Stay Indoors: Remain in an interior room or hallway away from windows and glass panes.
  • The "Eye" Deception: If winds suddenly stop, DO NOT go outside. The second half of the cyclone is often stronger.
  • Power & Gas: Switch off main circuit breakers and LPG cylinders before floodwaters enter.
  • Terrace Stranded: If water forces you onto a roof, take a white sheet and flashlight to signal SAR helicopters.
Flood & Sea Inundation (వరద రక్షణ సూత్రాలు)
  • Never Walk in Moving Water: Just 15 cm of moving floodwater can sweep a human off their feet.
  • Electrocution Risk: Avoid touching water where fallen cables or transformer boxes are submerged.
  • Contaminated Water: Boil all drinking water or use chlorine/halogen tablets. Do not drink tap water.
  • Dry Elevation: Keep children, pregnant mothers, and medication elevated on concrete beds.
First Aid: CPR & Cardiac (గుండెపోటు ప్రథమ చికిత్స)
  • Unresponsive Victim: Check responsiveness. Call 108 immediately.
  • Hands-Only CPR: Push hard and fast in the center of the chest (100–120 beats/min).
  • Chest Compression Depth: Compress at least 5 cm deep; allow full chest recoil between compressions.
  • Recovery Position: If breathing but unconscious, turn them onto their side to keep airway open.
First Aid: Severe Bleeding & Snakebite (రక్తస్రావం / పాముకాటు)
  • Severe Bleeding: Apply continuous direct firm pressure using a clean cloth or bandage. Elevate limb.
  • Snakebite: Keep the bitten limb completely still below heart level. DO NOT cut, suck venom, or apply tight tourniquets.
  • Antivenom Transport: Rush victim immediately to King George Hospital (KGH) or VIMS where antivenom is stocked.
